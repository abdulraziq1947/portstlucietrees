Tree Care Prevention

If you have a property that has trees on it now, or you are planning on growing trees, preventative care is absolutely necessary. If you currently have tress growing, taking care of them so that they don’t die, and then cause harm to your poverty or person, is extremely important. If you are planting new trees, and trying to get them to grow nicely, protecting them so that they can get full-sized should be a priority. No matter what type of care you are needing, we are there for you.

Pruning

If you are lucky enough to have your trees in bloom, and the foliage is getting bigger, and bigger, it may be time to bring us in to prune. While a nice, full, canopy can make your trees look absolutely beautiful, it can also cause issues. Dry leaves falling can create fire hazards on the ground. Leaves stacking up can also increase the load weight of branches, eventually making them snap and plummet to the ground below. For these reasons, keeping your canopy properly prunes is an essential part of tree ownership.

Branch Removal

Branch removal, and trimming, goes hand in hand with pruning. As branches become bigger, and the foliage they carry, becomes more dense, more stress is put on to the branch. While, typically, branches can support themselves, outside forces, such as rain or wind, can cause this stress to amplify. When that happens, branches can snap and break off. This is a major danger for anything, or anyone, around your trees. In order to remediate this situation, the best thing to do is to have branches trimmed and maintained. Likewise, if we assess that certain branches have died, they should immediately be removed, completely, or run the risk of falling off.

Mulching

Mulching is a process in which mulch is paced at the base of your tree to help it grow strong. In this process, leaves are usually raked away from the gas of your tree and the top layer of earth is dug out around the trunk. Once this happens, We use mulch to fill in that depression. Mulch helps to properly siphon water through, to your trunks root system. Mulch also helps to protect the base of the trunk, and keep is sturdy. Mulching newer trees will be a huge benefit to having them grow quickly.

Small Tree Protection

Another service that we offer is the protection of small trees. Because saplings are prime treats for all manner of critters, it is important to have them protected while they are trying to grow. These tiny trees are also at the mercy of nearly any inclement weather system. For these reasons, young trees need a little hep to fully grow up. We can come in and build a cage around your tree; effectively blocking out would be predators. We can also install a brace alongside your tree, so that it will stand up through winds.
