Tree Trimming & Pruning

Pruning is how you keep a tree you want. The job is to take weight off the roof side, clear the walk, and remove dead wood. It is not a excuse to cut the tree into a pole.

Call 772-492-4436

When to call

  • Limbs rub the roof, the gutter, or the pool cage.
  • Dead wood is hanging over where people walk.
  • The canopy is so thick that wind cannot pass through it.
  • You can see a crack at a crotch and want it assessed before hurricane season.

How the work goes

  1. Walk the tree and mark what comes off: dead wood, roof clearance, and weight.
  2. Make cuts at a branch collar, not flush against the trunk and not halfway out a limb.
  3. Lower pieces that would hit the house.
  4. Chip the brush and leave the tree with a crown, not a hat rack.

What changes the price

  • How much live wood has to come off.
  • Whether the work is over a roof.
  • The size of the tree and the number of trees.
  • Haul-away of the brush.

Questions about this job

Will trimming keep a tree from falling in a hurricane?

No trim makes a tree storm-proof. Removing dead wood and end weight gives wind less to pull on. A hollow trunk or a bad lean is a removal, not a pruning job.

How much of the tree will you cut?

Enough to solve the problem you called about. Taking more than about a quarter of the live crown in one year stresses the tree. If someone offers to top it, that is the wrong job.

When is a good time to prune in Port St. Lucie?

Dead wood can come off any month. Live pruning is easier to schedule before hurricane season, while the crew can still get to you. Do not wait until a storm is already named.

Can you trim a tree away from my neighbor's yard?

We can cut limbs that are over your side when we are hired to work the tree. A dispute about whose tree it is should be settled before we cut live wood on someone else's trunk.
