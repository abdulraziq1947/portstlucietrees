Storm Damage Clean Up

Florida is a great state to live in and, most of the time, we have incredibly beautiful weather. However, one of the few drawbacks is that Florida is prone to storms sweeping through. While most of these storms will do little damage, save for intense rain, some will wreak havoc. If a storm moves through, with strong winds, it is very likely that any trees that you have on your property will be rocked pretty hard. These types of storms can lead to all sorts of debris being blown about.

Fallen Limbs

Fallen limbs are one of the main consequences of a storm passing through your property. While larger branches may be able to withstand, even the heaviest of winds, smaller branches will not. When the wind is too much for branches to handle, it will cause them to splinter, break and blow in to your property. This can cause harm to other things on your property, but hopefully it doesn’t. If you are noticing several branches, strewn about, after a storm passes through, give us a call so that we can come and clean up for you.

Needle and Leaf Cleanup

Needles and leaves from trees are things that absolutely cannot withstand storm winds. Heavy rains and winds will cause these things to dislodge and fall to the ground en masse. As these pieces of debris fall, they will, no doubt, get caught up in the winds and be blown all over. Raking and cleaning this type of debris up can take a long time. For this reason, we would love to be the people to do it for you. Rather than take time out of your schedule to rake up all the leaves and needles that are spread out over your property, let us take care of it for you.

Broken Trees

Broken trees are a little harder to clean up after a storm. If the storm was big enough to knock over fully grown trees, you may be having other things to worry about. If this is the case, please let us know so that you can get back to taking care of yourself, and let us worry about the trees. If trees are completely down, we can cut them in to pieces, for you to use, or help in selling them to companies that will buy them from you, to use in other products.

Other Debris

There is no telling what other kind of debris could be spread around after a storm. If you give us the call to come out and help, we can pick up anything that is not supposed to be there. While our focus is mainly on natural debris, we can assist in the cleaning up of nearly anything. With this process, we just want to make sure that your property is cleaned and that, as a result, you are kept safe. Anytime you need a storm clean up, let us be your first, and only, call.
