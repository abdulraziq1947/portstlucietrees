Stump Removal

The tree is gone and the stump is still in the lawn. It catches the mower, sprouts, and stays a trip. Grinding takes it below the grass line.

Call 772-492-4436

When to call

  • You are still steering the mower around a stump from an older removal.
  • Shoots keep coming up around the old trunk.
  • You want to replant, pour a walk, or put a fence through that spot.
  • The stump is near a drive and people trip on it at night.

How the work goes

  1. Check for irrigation, utilities flags, and how close the stump is to concrete.
  2. Grind the stump and the flare of the roots down below grade.
  3. Rake the chips. You can keep them as mulch or we can haul them.
  4. The hole settles. Top it with soil before you lay sod.

What changes the price

  • Diameter of the stump.
  • How many stumps are on the property.
  • Access for the grinder.
  • Whether chips are hauled away.
  • Rock, metal, or concrete hidden in the wood.

Questions about this job

Do you grind the stump the same day as the removal?

Often yes, if the grinder can get in and the estimate included it. A soft yard after rain may need a second trip so we do not rut the lawn.

Will grinding kill the roots?

It removes the stump and the roots next to it. Roots farther out stay in the ground and decay. You should not expect every root in the yard to disappear.

Can you grind a stump next to a sidewalk?

Usually, if we can keep the machine off the concrete. Tell us about irrigation lines. A line under the stump can be cut by the teeth.

Is the grind area ready for grass?

Not the same day. The chips and the hole need soil on top. We leave the spot lower than the lawn so you can fill it, or we can talk about that as part of the job.
