Commercial Tree Service

A commercial property is a work site, not a backyard. Cars, customers, and other trades are already there. The cut has to be scheduled, coned off, and finished so the lot can reopen.

Call 772-492-4436

When to call

  • A tree is over a parking lot, a sign, or a roof.
  • A job site has to be cleared before construction.
  • An HOA common area has deadwood over a sidewalk.
  • Storm debris is blocking access for tenants.

How the work goes

  1. Walk the site with you and note hours when the lot can be closed.
  2. Protect cars, glass, and pedestrian paths before any cutting.
  3. Remove or prune the trees in the estimate and keep a clear exit.
  4. Haul debris the same day if the lot cannot hold a pile.

What changes the price

  • Number and size of trees.
  • Whether the work is at night or while the business is open.
  • Traffic control and how close the public can get.
  • Haul-away and any required permit.

Questions about this job

Can you work before we open?

Often, if the light and the noise rules allow it. Tell us the hours. A cut over a full parking lot costs more because every limb has to be lowered, not dropped.

Do you carry insurance for a commercial site?

Ask us for the current certificate before we start. We will send what the property manager needs. Do not take an old photocopy from a different year as proof.

Will you follow an HOA or property-manager spec?

Yes. Send the written request, including trees that must stay. We price that scope. We do not add trees because they look easy once we are on site.

Can you clear a commercial lot?

Yes. Lot clearing for a build is listed as its own service because the volume is different from pruning a parking-lot oak. We can price both if the site needs both.
