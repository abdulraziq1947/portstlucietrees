Tree Removal

Have the tree taken down while you can still choose the day. A dead trunk, a lean toward the roof, or roots lifting the walk is easier to handle before wind does it for you.

Call 772-492-4436

When to call

  • The trunk is dead, hollow, or cracked, or a large limb is already hanging.
  • The tree leans over the house, the pool, or the neighbor's roof.
  • Roots are lifting the driveway, the sidewalk, or the foundation slab.
  • You are building, fencing, or opening the yard and the tree is in the way.

How the work goes

  1. Look at the tree, the drop zone, and how the truck gets in.
  2. Set ropes and cut from the top when the tree is close to a structure.
  3. Lower the trunk in sections and keep the wood off the roof and walls.
  4. Chip the brush, haul the logs, and rake the work area.

What changes the price

  • Height and trunk diameter.
  • How close the tree is to the house, fence, pool, or wires.
  • Access for the truck and chipper.
  • Whether logs are hauled away or left for firewood.
  • Stump grinding, and a permit if the tree is protected.

Questions about this job

How do you remove a tree next to a house?

The tree is dismantled from the top. Limbs are roped down and the trunk is cut in short sections so nothing free-falls onto the roof. Open yards can sometimes take a single fell. The method is chosen from the site, not from a photo of a different yard.

Will you remove a tree that is touching power lines?

No. A tree in a utility line is a power-company problem until the line is made safe. Call the utility first. We can remove the tree after the line is clear.

Do you haul the wood away?

Yes, if that is part of the estimate. Some homeowners keep the logs. Brush is normally chipped and removed so the yard is not left full of limbs.

How long does a removal take?

A straightforward residential tree is often finished the same day. A large tree over a roof, or a yard the truck cannot enter, takes longer. The estimate includes the time, not a guess from the street.

What should I do before the crew arrives?

Move cars out of the drive, open a gate if there is one, and keep people and pets inside while the saws are running. Tell us about dogs, septic fields, and irrigation heads before we drive on the lawn.
